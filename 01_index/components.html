Lottie

Animated media (.about__media-lottie) — used on the About page. data-lottie-src is the idle loop; data-lottie-actions lists one-shot animations, each with a label. Hover shows the next label in the cursor circle ("Feed", then "Spin", then back to "Feed"); a click plays it and the face returns to idle. Only the pause/play button (bottom right) shows "Pause"/"Play". Reduced-motion visitors get the still first frame until they press play. Add data-lottie-trigger="hover" to play the actions on hover with no cursor label instead — the nav logo does this to spin once.
