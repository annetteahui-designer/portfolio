Background

I've been passionate about art for as long as I can remember. When it came time to choose a career path, I stumbled upon design. Design was an unfamiliar territory, but I knew I wanted to create things that connected with people. One thing led to another, and a whole new passion bloomed.

Today, I am visual designer with a BFA in Design from the University of Texas at Austin whose career has become multifaceted and fueled by curiosity. I love exploring new creative avenues, picking up new skills, and seeing where each project takes me.
