Layland RV Park & Campsite

Design Areas

  • custom_typography Branding

Team

Annette Hui, Chris Rodriguez, Dev Gupta

Layland, a RV park and campground with locations spanning across NY, MA, and PA, offers a glamping experience that blends comfort with adventure.

The rebranding aimed to capture this essence, ensuring it remained inclusive and welcoming, rather than exclusively targeting upscale clientele. Drawing inspiration from brands like Sweetgreen, the color palette exuded vibrant and inviting energy. Iconography and visuals were deliberately crafted with thick, rounded lines to evoke friendliness and approachability.

Website

The website mirrors Sweet Green's color palette, featuring forest green accented with vibrant pops of electric yellow.

Print material & way finding

Physical material exists all across the campgrounds. From handbooks, menus, to signage, a system was defined to ensure consistency.

Icons

Previously, the brand used custom-drawn icons, but it was determined, they didn't need to be overly stylized. To streamline the process, we sourced icons from the Google Icon Library, opting for thicker-filled forms for a bold look.

Additionally, custom icons tailored to specific locations, ensuring a cohesive visual language across the brand.

Social Media

The proposed aesthetic for Layland's social media channels combine the brand's photography, catchy phrases, and distinctive badges, creating a cohesive and engaging visual identity.

These badges are a neat way to spruce Layland's identity. They can be used for social media tiles, be turned into stickers, or even be placed on top of photos.
