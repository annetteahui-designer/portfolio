The DocuSign Developer Library

Design Areas

  • custom_typography Branding
  • edit Illustration

Team

Annette Hui, Dev Gupta

The DocuSign Library is a tool kit housing templates, illustrations, and color palettes curated for the DocuSign team's graphic design needs. It offers user-friendly accessibility and is readily made for various design purposes.

Experience a sneak peek of this cutting-edge design system in this captivating video!

Social Media Templates

These designs were tailored specifically for the in-house DocuSign team, streamlining their graphic creation process. With these templates, team members could effortlessly generate graphics by selecting an illustration and adding a title.

Ideal for video covers, advertisements, and social media posts, these templates have proven to be invaluable assets.

Illustrations

The illustration library boasts an extensive collection of over 100+ abstract illustrations: squares, circles, arrows, documents.

Color Palettes

The design library features a meticulously curated color system. Each color palette includes vibrant pops of neon colors.

Micro Animations

Micro animations were created to highlight DocuSign Developer's UI. These could be used to create engaging visuals for marketing materials.

DEVCON

This design library and system played a pivotal role in shaping the branding for DEVCON, the 2021 DocuSign Developer Conference.

The campaign was vibrant and gradient-driven creating a striking visual identity. Visual assets ranged from the website, social media, to even swag.
