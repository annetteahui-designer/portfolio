### Zaum (2) — `/zaum-1`

ZAUM's logo works to also exist in unique environments and does so by tagging and vandalism.

### Go Grow a Home — `/gogrow`
**Go Grow a Home** — Identity System

Go Grow a Home is a conceptual campaign project aimed to challenge families in Austin to grow at home especially when it can be an inexpensive, fun, and great bonding experience for the whole family.

By using collage, cut out illustrations, and hand written material, the design is accessible. An analogy to how this at-home project can be accessible for Austin families as well.

### Butterflyy — `/butterflyy`
**butterflyy** — Identity System

Butterflyy is a conceptualized clothing brand that repurposes thrifted clothes by screen printing butterfly patterns- a transformative experience. Instead of branding them with the same repetitive logo, it breaks up letterforms from the logo and turns them into many different butterfly patterns. This adds an exciting approach to how thrifted clothes can be renewed.

### Snake Heads — `/snakeheads`
**SNAKE HEADS** — Typography and Graphic Design

SNAKE HEADS is a punk band of two sisters that like to "BITE THE HEAD OFF THE WORLD".

The graphics made required a personalized modular typeface, so a whole alphabet was made for this project. The process included scanning, hand written material, and ripping for a very punk aesthetic.

### Pond Ecosystem — `/pond2`

The Pond Ecosystem card game is a card game meant to teach kids… well the pond ecosystem of course! Each card is a letter of an animal or plant with a fun fact placed on the back of the card.

A poster was created as a supplement to the cards and serves as a fun visual to see all the animals and plants together.

Lastly, these cards would exist in science museums and nature park stores.
